## What was and was not verified

The UPSC archive lists the two 2016 objective papers separately from the conventional papers. The official PDFs open as PDF resources, but screenshot/page retrieval returns cache errors in this execution environment. Direct binary downloads also fail. Therefore no page-by-page comparison of the secondary copies against the official scans has been completed.

The answer keys printed in the ACE copies are secondary coaching keys. They are not authenticated UPSC answer keys. They must not be labelled official or treated as independently verified merely because they appear beside the questions.

No 2016 questions or answer keys have been admitted to the production dataset. No complete official-source audit has been claimed.

## 2010–2015

Secondary listings and download leads remain in `data/source_inventory.json`. A listing is not proof that a file is the correct ESE Civil technical objective paper. The 2010–2015 paper files and answer keys still require retrieval, paper identity confirmation and page-by-page audit.

## Copyright and repository admission

The repository is public. Full verbatim republication of complete third-party question papers and coaching solutions requires a clear reuse licence or permission. The portal can link to source PDFs and store independently created metadata, verification status, topic tags and original explanations. Do not bulk-copy copyrighted solved-paper books into the public repository without permission.
