## Important paper-structure note

The UPSC archive explicitly lists two Civil Engineering **Objective Papers** for ESE 2016: Paper I and Paper II. Both are in scope as technical Civil Engineering objective papers. The separate conventional/descriptive papers are excluded. The 2016 PDFs are linked directly above.

Do not assume that the paper structure or paper count is identical across all years. Verify each year against its own official notification and source documents before importing questions.

## Import status

No questions have been transcribed from these PDFs yet. No answer keys have been verified or imported. The two 2016 PDFs are source candidates only; they must be downloaded and checked page by page before any question records are admitted.

### Reuse and integrity rule

These sites can reduce discovery and comparison effort, but they do not replace official UPSC papers. Do not bulk-copy their question text or solutions into this project without checking source provenance, accuracy and applicable reuse/licensing terms. Third-party answer keys remain provisional until independently verified.
