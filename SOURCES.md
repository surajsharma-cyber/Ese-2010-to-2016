## Important paper-structure note

The UPSC archive explicitly lists two Civil Engineering **Objective Papers** for ESE 2016: Paper I and Paper II. Both are in scope as technical Civil Engineering objective papers. The separate conventional/descriptive papers are excluded. The 2016 PDFs are linked directly above.

Do not assume that the paper structure or paper count is identical across all years. Verify each year against its own official notification and source documents before importing questions.
